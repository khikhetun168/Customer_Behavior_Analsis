# Customer_Behavior_Analsis
This project analyzes retail customer behavior and shopping trends using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

The goal is to simulate a real-world Data Analyst workflow where raw retail data is transformed into meaningful business insights that can support decision-making in areas such as customer segmentation, product performance, purchasing behavior, subscriptions, discounts, and sales trends.

-- ============================================================
-- BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
-- ============================================================

-- Q1. How does total revenue differ between male and female customers?

-- Q2. Which customers used a discount but still spent more
--     than the average purchase amount?

-- Q3. Which are the top 5 products with the highest
--     average review rating?

-- Q4. How do average purchase amounts differ between
--     Standard and Express shipping?

-- Q5. Do subscribed customers spend more than
--     non-subscribed customers?

-- Q6. Which 5 products have the highest percentage
--     of purchases made with discounts?

-- Q7. How many customers are New, Returning, and Loyal
--     based on their previous purchases?

-- Q8. What are the top 3 most purchased products
--     within each category?

-- Q9. Are repeat buyers (more than 5 previous purchases)
--     also likely to subscribe?

-- Q10. Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
